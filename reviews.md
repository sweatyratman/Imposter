# Market research: what players love, hate and want in impostor games

*Compiled 22 Oct 2026 by four research agents: Google Play reviews, App Store reviews, community discussion, and house rules and variants.*

**How solid is the evidence?**
- **The research environment couldn't open store pages or Reddit directly.** Store and Reddit pages, plus most other hosts, were blocked or failed to resolve. The evidence comes from search-engine extracts of App Store and Play listings and reviews, review aggregators (AppBrain, mwm.ai, game-solver, Similarweb), BoardGameGeek threads, official rulebooks, and party-game guides.
- **Quotes are search snippets.** They are as close to verbatim as was available but unverified. Treat them as indicative.
- **Each finding is tagged:** **(recurring)** means many reviews or several apps; **(single)** means one person.
- **No Reddit evidence exists.** To get it, paste thread links into a session or run from a machine that can reach reddit.com. Good places to look: r/boardgames, r/partygames, r/socialdeduction, r/CasualUK.

---

## 1. Headline findings

1. **Paywalls and subscriptions are the genre's number one complaint (recurring, every app).**
   - Weekly subscriptions run $4.99–$7.99.
   - Free tiers have 1–5 categories.
   - Trials auto-renew, and refunds get refused.
   - Players say, verbatim: "I will NOT pay for a subscription", "almost everything costs money", "not worth eight dollars a month".
2. **Repeated words are the number one content complaint (recurring, every app, paid tiers included).**
   - "we kept getting repeats"
   - "u can run out of words and u wont be able to play the game anymore"
   - the same word served back to back
3. **The market leader wins on no ads and a one-off price.**
   - *Imposter Game – Party Edition* has 4.8★ from about 94K iOS ratings: no ads, and a $2.99 or $9.99 lifetime unlock.
   - Its one 1★-style story ("got scammed") came when a later "PRO" tier re-locked content a lifetime buyer had already paid for.
4. **The most-loved qualities are offline play, one phone, no sign-up, and being good for family, kids, car rides and getting people off their phones (recurring).**
5. **Online lobbies generate the worst reviews (recurring).**
   - Empty lobbies: "not a single person joined within 30 minutes".
   - Hosts kicking players, toxic chat, ads between games.
   - Pass-the-phone avoids all of this.
6. **People want control over impostor numbers, not chaos.**
   - "would be great if we could set a range for it - e.g. 'between 1 and 3' imposters… randomly having 5 imposters in a 6-player game would be a waste of everyone's time" (Party Edition, Play).
7. **Fairness complaints:**
   - The impostor speaking first has "no leverage" and is "likely to lose automatically".
   - The same person keeps being the impostor.
   - Ties favour the impostor at 4 players.

**Where Impostor already stands:**
- Free, no ads, offline, one phone, no account.
- All 20 categories free; 1,300+ words.
- Word and clue history to avoid repeats.
- Four rotating clues, which fixes "hints that don't make sense or don't match".
- Odds sliders with a max, which is the "range" people ask for.
- Fooled impostors (Undercover-style) and no-clue mode (close to Mr White).
- A caught impostor's guess with its own house rule.
- Scoring with an underdog bonus.
- Region word lists.

**Gaps, roughly in order of evidence:**
1. Starter fairness.
2. Mr White / blank card.
3. Tie handling.
4. Back-to-back impostor protection.
5. Sound on the reveal.
6. Custom categories (paid, one-off).
7. More modes (question mode, drawing).
8. Word difficulty tiers.

## 3. What players love (most frequent first)

1. **Offline, one phone, no account (recurring, all apps):** bus rides, holidays, family nights, bedtime. "works offline super efficient".
2. **Bringing people together / family-friendly (recurring):**
   - "played the whole vacation with family, parents loved it"
   - "a good family game for keeping kids off their phones while they still feel like they're on them"
   - "good way to disconnect from phones"
3. **No ads or a one-off price (recurring for Party Edition):** "good to have a one-time payment option instead of a subscription".
4. **Simplicity, instant laughs (recurring):** "very user friendly". The TikTok trend's fun comes from near-misses: an impostor guessing the word by accident, or a crewmate who thinks it's "carrot".
5. **An optional hint for the impostor, especially with kids:** it "makes the imposter feel included" (single, but it matches complaints when hints are missing).
6. **Custom categories with your own hints (recurring):** loved, but people resent paying for them ("I'd love if the custom categories were free").
7. **Special roles and variety:** Mr White and Undercover's roles are the signature of the most durable app.
8. **Responsive developers who fix bugs quickly.**

## 6. Game-design problems players report, and fixes

| Problem | Evidence | Fixes in the wild | Impostor status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The impostor speaks first with nothing to go on** | Chameleon ("likely to lose automatically"), Spyfall ("no leverage") | Random starter that never favours the impostor; **impostor/Mr White never starts**; **hint only if the impostor starts**; everyone shouts clues at once; write clues and reveal together | Starter is random, which isn't checked against role. **Gap: add a house rule** |
| Impostor guesses too easily in big groups | Chameleon 7–8; Fake Artist | Hide the category after clues (official Chameleon 7–8 variant); no hint | ✅ hide-category toggle exists |
| Impostor can't win in small groups | Chameleon 3p; Spyfall | **Two guesses at 3 players** (official Chameleon); hint on; two clue laps | Gap: consider "2 guesses at 3 players" |
| Word too easy or too obscure | Insider ("too fragile") | Pick from 3; swap a word; **difficulty tiers** (Werewords) | ⏳ difficulty tiers (backlog #34) |
| Clues too vague or too obvious | All | Teach it | ✅ how-to-play scene |
| Endless discussion in big groups | Chameleon 8p (3-min timer); Spyfall timer "fiddly" | **Optional** timer, never forced | ✅ clue timer |
| **Ties** | Chameleon 4p "often a tie… favoured the Chameleon" | Re-vote between tied (Chinese "PK"); dealer decides; tie = impostor escapes (one house) | Gap: backlog #21 |
| Caught early, then bored | Spyfall | Always score; reward a late guess | ✅ scoring |
| Rounds feel samey with no climax | Chameleon reviews | Scoring across rounds, first to N | ✅ scores carry over; ⏳ consider "first to N" |
| Same person keeps being impostor | Apps + BGG | Re-deal for a back-to-back impostor; better randomness | Gap: soft weighting (careful with the no-leak rule) |

## 7. Modes and house rules worth offering (ranked by player love × simplicity)

1. **Fair starter**: "the impostor never goes first", or "give a hint only if the impostor starts". One toggle, the most-cited fairness complaint.
2. **Caught impostor's final guess** ✅. Extras: two guesses at 3 players; optional **multiple-choice guess** (like Out of the Loop and Chameleon's 16-word grid), which removes arguments over near-misses and fits our "no typed input" rule.
3. **Impostor count range plus odds** ✅. Extra: "impostors know each other" (Spyfall 2 and Party Edition's Team mode) for 2+ impostors.
4. **Undercover similar word** ✅ (fooled impostors).
5. **Mr White / blank card**: no word and no clue, final guess. The most-loved third role. Should never start. (Backlog #46.)
6. **Another lap of clues** (backlog #15). Fake Artist plays two laps by default.
7. **Optional timer** ✅.
8. **Tie handling**: re-vote between the tied players (backlog #21).
9. **Scoring options**: an **accuser bonus** (Spyfall: +1 for whoever started the right accusation); "guess-steal pays less" (Chameleon gives 1 instead of 2); allow score-free play.
10. **Hide category after clues** in big groups ✅ (via the hide-category toggle).
11. **Back-to-back impostor protection**: invisible soft weighting.
12. **Paid-bundle modes**: Werewords-style **Seer/detective** (backlog #45, very well loved), Insider-style yes/no questions, **question mode** (everyone answers the same question except one: Party Edition's "Find the Liar", Out of the Loop), **drawing** (Fakeit, Imposter Up), Undercover's special roles (tie-breaker, lovers, revenger, mime).
13. **Word difficulty tiers** (Werewords' Easy, Medium, Hard, Ridiculous are the best-praised fix).
14. **Drinking variants** exist (whoever voted out an innocent drinks, or the losing side drinks). This could be an 18+ pack's flavour, not core.

## 8. Word and clue design (for content work)

- **A good secret word** is familiar enough that everyone can describe it, but broad enough that clues don't give it away. Skip anything someone would need explained. Abstract-but-familiar words only suit experienced groups. The Chameleon's words are sometimes "too common", which makes hiding hard.
- **Undercover word-pair rules** (Chinese 谁是卧底 guides). Apply them when choosing fooled-impostor alternatives:
  - related but not the same;
  - neither contains the other (furniture/table is bad);
  - same part of speech;
  - not two names for one thing;
  - many shared features but enough difference. Good pairs: wire/fibre cable, cricket/katydid, camera/video camera.
- **Difficulty pairs:** Coffee/Espresso is hard; Sun/Moon is easy.
- **Custom lists** should have at least 20 words to avoid repeats (one guide).
- **Mixed-culture tables** struggle with culture-specific topics. ✅ Regions address this.

## 9. Player counts (consensus)

- **Sweet spot:** 4–10 players (5–8 per some guides). Chameleon "only really shows its true colours with five or more"; 4 players ties a lot; 8 drags.
- **Impostors by group size:** roughly 1 for 3–5 players, 2 for 6–8 (or 8+), 2–3 for 9–12, 3–4 for 13–20, or about 1 per four players. Chinese 谁是卧底: about 1 undercover per 4–5 players, plus an optional blank from about 8.
- ✅ `AUTO_ODDS` matches: a lone impostor at 3–5, leaning to 2 at about 8 and 3 at about 12.

## 10. Scoring systems in the wild

| Game | Hidden player | Others |
|---|---|---|
| Chameleon | escapes 2; caught but guesses 1; caught and wrong 0 | 2 each only if caught and wrong |
| Spyfall | 2 if nobody convicted; 4 if an innocent is convicted; 4 for a correct location guess | 1 each; **2 for the player who started the accusation** |
| Fake Artist | fake and question-master 2 each | 1 each |
| Undercover (Yanstar) | Undercover 10, Mr White 6 | 2 each |
| **Impostor (ours)** | 2 each, 3 for a lone impostor; caught 0 unless the guess wins | 1 each |

**Takeaway:** every scored game pays the lone hidden player more, so our underdog bonus matches the genre. Untried ideas: an accuser bonus, and reduced points for a guess-steal.

## 11. Monetisation lessons

| Model | User reaction |
|---|---|
| **No ads + one-off lifetime unlock** (Party Edition) | Best-rated, market leader; "one-time payment instead of a subscription" |
| **Ads + cheap one-off packs, any purchase removes ads** (Undercover) | Accepted; complaints are about ads and repeats, not prices |
| **Weekly or yearly subscriptions** (Imposter Who?, Fakeit, Imposter Up) | Most resentment: "I will NOT pay for a subscription", "not worth eight dollars a month", surprise charges |
| **Re-locking lifetime purchases** | "got scammed" |
| **Paywalled custom categories** | Seen as basic; resented |
| **Coins bought with real money** | Disliked |

**For Impostor:** the planned model (free core forever; one-off £0.99–1.99 packs; £4.99 everything bundle; no ads, no subscriptions) sits exactly in the zone users accept. Rules to keep:
- **Never re-lock a purchase.**
- **Never put core features behind payment.**
- **State "no ads, no subscriptions, no repeated words" on the store listing**, because these are the top three pain points of every competitor.
- **Consider a small free allowance of custom categories** (for example one list), with more as the paid unlock.
